Deploy the WATT LayerZero mesh

Owner tool. Deploys the self-hosted LayerZero V2 stack (endpoint + send/receive ULN + DVN worker) and the WattOFTAdapter on each chain, then wires peers and grants the adapter minter rights on WATT v2. Every step is a Rabby-signed transaction. Progress is saved locally, so you can stop and resume.

⚠️ Connect the wallet that should OWN the bridge — it becomes the LZ delegate/config owner and must also be the WATT v2 owner to grant minter rights. Deploy on Altcoinchain and Polygon (either order), then Set Peers + Grant Minter on both.
